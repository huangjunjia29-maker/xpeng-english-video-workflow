# Masters, Optional Expansion, and Copy Matching

## Plan and Approve Masters

1. Use the adopted brief's directions and evidence. Recommend 5-20 masters from the actual source quality and natural chapters; neither nine masters nor three per direction is a permanent rule.
2. Confirm video form, narration choice, visible source labels, orientation, and proposed allocation if not already decided for this batch.
3. Assign stable master IDs and record direction, hook, source intervals/images, narration if any, and approval status. Use supplied hooks first; extend within the same direction when needed, without inventing facts.
4. Approve a sample when introducing a new format, then create and QA the agreed masters. Keep review previews separate from final approved files.

## Optional Plugin Expansion

The user may expand approved masters with a plugin. Expansion is optional and its count is separately chosen; do not assume 90 variants. Preserve the master-to-direction record. Do not rename, move, or modify expanded files when the request is only to match copy.

## Match Every Expanded Video

- Inventory actual video files and determine the order the user will use to publish. For numbered filenames, use natural numeric order (`2` before `10`), not directory enumeration, upload time, or guessed master order. Record that order explicitly.
- Identify each variant's master/direction from reliable expansion metadata and audiovisual checks. If metadata is absent, compare representative frames, title/hook, content, and audio as appropriate against approved masters. Do not assume the expansion preserves master order or cycles predictably.
- Check every variant. For automated matching, use multiple representative positions and confidence/margin checks; manually inspect ambiguous cases and possible cross-direction mixtures. Do not claim certainty from filenames alone or from a single shared frame.
- Assign a unique post from the same direction that fits the actual video's evidence. Same-direction membership alone is insufficient if the post mentions a scene, feature, market, or claim absent from that video. Copy is a social caption, not necessarily a verbatim transcript.
- If there are too few suitable posts, resolve the gap before final delivery; do not duplicate posts or borrow from another direction merely to fill rows. Keep internal evidence and unresolved cases outside the publishing sheet.

## Publishing Workbook and QA

Export one `Copy` sheet: `No.`, `English`, `中文`, `视频文件名`. English stays in column B for one-step copying. Each row corresponds to exactly one actual video in the agreed order. Grouped review copy may be kept separately, but clearly identify the video-matched workbook as the publishing version; never reshuffle it after pairing.

Reopen the exported workbook and verify row count, exact filename/order, no missing or repeated video, unique copy usage, direction and content consistency, bilingual text, hashtags, and character limits. Report checked counts and any remaining uncertainty accurately. File matching does not authorize social publishing or unrelated cloud writes.
